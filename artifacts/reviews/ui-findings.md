Findings

1. **severity: high** | **screenshot/page:** `timeline.png` | **issue:** The six-track view is too dense: tiny labels (`ARM/REC/MUT/SOL`, lane tags, FX rows, right-corner glyphs) compete at the same visual weight, making scan time high and track state hard to parse quickly. | **brief suggested fix:** Increase typographic/spacing contrast by promoting key states (track name, armed/rec, active loop) and de-emphasizing secondary metadata with lighter color or smaller grouping density.

2. **severity: high** | **screenshot/page:** `timeline-focused.png` | **issue:** The top control row (`REC MODE`, `REC WARP`, `SONG LOOP`, etc.) mixes selected and unselected states with similar luminance, so current mode is not immediately obvious. | **brief suggested fix:** Strengthen state communication with a clearer selected style (higher contrast fill + stronger border/icon), and unify unselected controls to a quieter baseline.

3. **severity: medium** | **screenshot/page:** `routing.png` | **issue:** `INPUT FX` and `OUTPUT FX` blocks have similar structure but differing accent colors and dense inline controls (`+`, `SET`, `TGL`) that read as cryptic without hierarchy. | **brief suggested fix:** Add clearer section headers and control grouping (label, value, action), and replace ambiguous abbreviations where space allows (`TGL` -> `TOGGLE` or icon+tooltip pattern).

4. **severity: medium** | **screenshot/page:** `midi-io.png` | **issue:** Input/output device cards contain large empty body areas with minimal per-device status, making the page feel visually heavy but low-information. | **brief suggested fix:** Either compact card heights or surface meaningful status rows (port/channel/activity/default flags) inside cards to justify vertical space.

5. **severity: medium** | **screenshot/page:** `mappings.png` | **issue:** Table row density is very high; `TYPE/DEVICE/SOURCE/TARGET/SCOPE/ON` columns are readable individually but hard to scan as actions due to tight row spacing and minimal zebra separation. | **brief suggested fix:** Add stronger row grouping/zebra contrast and slightly larger vertical padding for faster line tracking.

6. **severity: medium** | **screenshot/page:** `mappings-overlay.png` | **issue:** Header metrics (`ROWS 1-19 / 30`, `SCOPE`) sit far right with weak visual linkage to the list below, so context feels detached. | **brief suggested fix:** Move or align summary metadata closer to table headers, or style as a compact stats bar directly above the list.

7. **severity: low** | **screenshot/page:** `timeline.png` and `timeline-focused.png` | **issue:** Some micro-labels appear near clipping thresholds (e.g., compact right-edge tokens and FX row suffixes), risking truncation on slightly smaller widths/scales. | **brief suggested fix:** Reserve minimum label widths and add truncation rules with ellipsis for non-critical text.

8. **severity: low** | **screenshot/page:** all pages | **issue:** Bottom hotkey hints (`F5 MAPPINGS`, `F7 DISCOVER`, `F8 DIRECT`) have low prominence relative to the rest of the frame and can be missed as primary navigation aids. | **brief suggested fix:** Increase contrast and spacing around global hotkey hints, and keep active page key visually emphasized consistently.
